🕉️ Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Atharva: The Divine is operated by Epsilonpi Limited, a New Zealand company ("we", "our", or "us"). It is a free Hindu devotional application built for the global community of devotees. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

1. Information We Collect

Atharva: The Divine does not require you to create an account or log in. We collect minimal information to operate the app:

  • Usage data: Anonymous data about which features are used (e.g. which screens are visited) may be collected through standard analytics tools. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • Device information: Basic device info (OS version, screen size) may be collected automatically to help us fix bugs and improve performance.
  • No personal data: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personally identifiable information.

2. YouTube Embedded Content

Atharva: The Divine displays devotional videos hosted on YouTube via YouTube's embedded player. When you watch a video, YouTube may collect data according to their own Privacy Policy. We do not control YouTube's data practices. By using the video features of this app, you agree to YouTube's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Information

Any anonymous usage data we collect is used solely to:

  • Understand which features are most useful to our community
  • Fix bugs and improve app performance
  • Plan future features and content additions

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Atharva: The Divine does not store personal data on any server. All app preferences (such as selected deity filters or audio settings) are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to us.

5. Children's Privacy

Atharva: The Divine is suitable for all ages and is frequently used by families to introduce children to devotional music and prayers. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases in the current version, and no social features.

6. Advertising

The current version of Atharva: The Divine contains no advertisements. If advertising is introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated and users will be informed.

7. Third-Party Services

Atharva: The Divine may use the following third-party services:

  • YouTube / Google: For embedded devotional video content
  • Expo (by Expo.dev): The framework powering the app's runtime

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy, which governs their data practices.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personally identifiable information, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. If you have concerns about data collected by YouTube or other embedded services, please refer to their respective privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
